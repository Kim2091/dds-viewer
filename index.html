FAQ

What is a DDS file?

DDS is a texture format commonly used by games and graphics applications. It can store compressed textures and multiple mipmap levels alongside ordinary image data.

Which formats are supported?

Image Viewer supports PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, AVIF, BMP, and DDS textures including BC1–BC5 / DXT1–DXT5, mipmaps, and cube maps.

How does conversion work?

Convert to… saves the visible image or selected DDS mip level and cube face in your chosen format. Convert Batch saves the first mip level of each DDS and every selected standard image into one ZIP archive.

How does Compare work?

Open a file, choose Compare, then select a second file. Use the slider to reveal each side. Both images share the same pan and zoom; DDS comparisons use the closest available mip level and cube face.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Files are read locally in your browser and are not sent to a server.

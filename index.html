FAQ

What is a DDS file?

DDS is a texture format commonly used by games and graphics applications. It can store compressed textures and multiple mipmap levels.

Which formats are supported?

DDS Viewer supports PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, AVIF, BMP, and DDS textures including BC1–BC5 / DXT1–DXT5, mipmaps, and cube maps.

How does PNG conversion work?

Export PNG saves the visible image or the selected DDS mip level and cube face. Batch Convert saves the first mip level of each DDS and every selected standard image into one ZIP archive.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Files are read locally in your browser and are not sent to a server.
